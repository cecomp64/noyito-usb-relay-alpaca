Boards detected on USB

Boards are identified by the USB port they are plugged into – keep each board in the same port. The 5-character board serial is set at the factory and often identical across boards.

Alpaca devices

Each device is one board. The device number is what clients such as NINA see (/api/v1/switch/N/…). Changes are saved immediately on Save and take effect without a restart; a client connected to a device whose hardware you change must reconnect.
